# Privacy policy

This policy covers **Quick Time Log & Edit for Everhour on Trello**, the Chrome extension ("the extension"). Last updated: 19 November 2026.

## Summary

The extension has no server, no analytics and no tracking. It shows and changes your own Everhour time entries on the Trello board you have open. It does this by talking to Everhour the same way Everhour's Trello Power-Up does. The developer never receives any of your data.

## What the extension accesses

It accesses the following only on a Trello board that uses the Everhour Power-Up, and only while the side panel is open:

- your Trello member ID and name, and the board's ID and name;
- the board's card names and lists, so each entry can show which card it belongs to;
- the board's Everhour time entries: date, duration, comment, card, member, and when the entry was made. The panel shows only your own.

To authorise each request to Everhour, the extension creates a short-lived sign-in token through Trello's Power-Up library. This is the same kind of token Everhour's Power-Up uses.

## Where data goes

- **To Everhour** (app.everhour.com). The extension reads your time entries, and saves the entries you log, edit, delete or undo. These are the same requests Everhour's own Power-Up sends.
- **To Trello**, through the Power-Up library that is already on the page. The extension reads card names, opens a card when you ask it to, and updates the card's Everhour time totals after a change, as Everhour does.

Nothing is sent to the developer or to any other third party. Data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not used for anything other than the panel's features.

## What the extension stores

It stores two settings in Chrome's local extension storage on your computer: the week you last viewed and your weekly target in hours.

Time entries and tokens stay in memory while the panel is open and are never written to disk. Uninstalling the extension removes the settings.
